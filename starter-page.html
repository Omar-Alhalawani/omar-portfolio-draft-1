Communities I Contribute To

I learn a lot from building with others. These roles combine engineering, organization, and serving the people around me.

Carleton University MSA (CUMSA)

Vice President of Administration — 2024 – Present

I help maintain the technical and organizational backbone for a 500+ student Muslim community at Carleton.

  • Manage and improve the CUMSA website, Discord server, and internal tools.
  • Design verification flows and announcement structures so information reaches the right people.
  • Support other execs in translating event ideas into reliable sign-up and communication systems.

Carleton Planetary Robotics Team (CPRT)

FPGA Subteam — 2025 – Present

Working with a 50+ member team on rover designs for competitions like URC/CIRC.

  • Develop Verilog modules and FPGA logic for rover subsystems.
  • Help integrate hardware designs with higher-level software and control systems.
  • Collaborate with mechanical, electrical, and software subteams to meet competition constraints.

BioCARE (Biomedical Carleton Applied Research & Engineering)

Software Team — 2025 – Present

Supporting interdisciplinary biomedical projects that bring together engineering and life sciences.

  • Contribute software and tooling support for biomedical prototypes and experiments.
  • Work with students from different disciplines to align technical work with real health-related needs.
