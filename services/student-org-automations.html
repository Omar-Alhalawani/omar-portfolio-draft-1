Student Org Tech & Automations

Helping student organizations stay organized with simple, robust digital systems.

Student organizations often juggle events, forms, chats, and announcements across different platforms. I help design structures and small automations that reduce chaos and make it easier for members to stay engaged.

What This Includes

  • Discord server layouts with clear channels and role structures.
  • Verification flows to keep spaces safe and trustworthy.
  • Google Forms/Sheets setups for events, volunteers, and feedback.
  • Lightweight bots or scripts to connect forms to chat platforms.

Perspective

Good systems don’t replace people—they support them. My goal is to reduce friction, so exec teams can spend more time on real community work and less on manual admin tasks.

Service Snapshot

  • Role: VP Administration (CUMSA)
  • Platforms: Discord, Google Workspace, simple automations
  • Best For: Student clubs and campus groups
