Embedded & FPGA Prototyping

Help with turning digital design ideas into working hardware prototypes.

I provide support for small teams and individuals who are working on embedded or FPGA-based projects, especially in academic or competition contexts. This can range from designing Verilog modules to helping debug timing or pin-mapping issues.

What I Can Help With

  • Designing and reviewing Verilog modules for clarity and synthesis.
  • Setting up simulation workflows (ModelSim/Vivado) for testing.
  • Creating and debugging FPGA constraints files.
  • Integrating low-level interfaces (SPI, I²C, UART, PWM, GPIO).

Who This Is For

Students, small project teams, or clubs who are building digital hardware and want a second pair of eyes on the logic, structure, or implementation details.

Service Snapshot

  • Focus: Verilog, FPGA, digital logic
  • Typical Context: Labs, course projects, competitions
  • Location: Remote or Ottawa-based
