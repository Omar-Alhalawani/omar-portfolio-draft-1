Electronics Repair & Optimization

Careful diagnostics and repair work with clear communication at every step.

I offer repair and tuning for laptops, desktops, and similar devices. This includes both software and hardware troubleshooting, with a focus on stability and transparency.

Typical Services

  • Operating system cleanup, reinstall, and performance optimization.
  • Hardware diagnostics and component replacement where feasible.
  • Data backup guidance before repairs that carry risk.
  • Thermal cleanup and repasting to restore performance.

How I Work

I document what I do in plain language and keep clients updated during the repair. The goal is not just to fix the device, but to make sure the owner understands what went wrong and how to prevent issues in the future.

  • Focus: Reliability, documentation, communication
