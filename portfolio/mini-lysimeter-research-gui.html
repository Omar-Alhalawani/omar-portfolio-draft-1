Mini-Lysimeter Research GUI

Multithreaded data acquisition and visualization tool for soil mechanics experiments.

The Mini-Lysimeter Research GUI is a Python-based system that integrates an NI-DAQ 9219 module and an Arduino to capture sensor data at 100 Hz. It was built to support soil mechanics experiments in a civil engineering lab.

The interface uses Tkinter and Matplotlib to provide synchronized multi-sensor plots, start/stop controls, and a clear view of experiment progress. Under the hood, multithreading keeps acquisition responsive while the GUI stays smooth.

Project Info

  • Role: Engineering Office & Laboratory Assistant
  • Tech: Python, Tkinter, Matplotlib, NI-DAQ 9219, Arduino
  • Year: 2025

Key Features

  • Multithreaded acquisition loop at 100 Hz for multiple channels.
  • Real-time plots with synchronized axes and auto-scaling.
  • Configurable autosave intervals with timestamped CSV files.
  • Designed for zero-loss long-term logging during multi-hour experiments.

Impact

The tool replaced manual recording and ad-hoc scripts with a single, reliable interface. It reduced setup time, made experiments easier to monitor, and produced clean datasets ready for analysis without extra cleanup steps.
