CUMSA Technical Infrastructure

Digital systems and automations built to support a large student community.

As Vice President of Administration for the Carleton University Muslim Students' Association (CUMSA), I help design and maintain the digital backbone for a 500+ member community.

This includes Discord server architecture, role systems, verification flows, event sign-up forms, and various small automations that keep information organized and accessible.

Project Info

  • Role: VP Administration, technical lead
  • Tools: Discord, Google Workspace, simple bots/scripts
  • Scope: 500+ students

Systems Work

  • Structured Discord channels and roles for clarity and safety.
  • Designed verification and onboarding flows for new members.
  • Set up forms and tracking sheets for volunteers and events.
  • Implemented lightweight automations to reduce manual admin work.

Why It Matters

Student orgs run on trust and clarity. Good technical infrastructure makes it easier for people to participate, find what they need, and feel supported by the community.
